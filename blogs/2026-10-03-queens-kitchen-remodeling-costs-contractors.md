Planning a kitchen remodel in Queens often starts with cabinets, counters, and layout ideas, but the borough's housing stock quickly brings the building itself into the conversation. A prewar co-op, an attached house, and a two-family home may require very different approvals, construction methods, and contractor logistics even when the kitchens are similar in size.
