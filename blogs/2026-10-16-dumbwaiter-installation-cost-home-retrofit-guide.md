You want groceries to get from the garage to the kitchen without a trip up the stairs, and you want to know what that costs before you call anyone. In an existing home, a residential dumbwaiter runs $10,000 to $30,000 installed, and most of that total goes into the house rather than the lift. The equipment is the easy part.
