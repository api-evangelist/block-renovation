When your house needs another bathroom, you have two ways to get one: build outward with an addition, or convert space you already have. Converting a bedroom into a bathroom makes a strong case for itself, especially when the room is barely used. That room already sits on the bedroom floor, steps from the rooms that need a second bathroom most.
