A wall of built-in bookshelves gives you the storage you've been missing without taking up the floor space of several freestanding units. It's also one of the most effective ways to make an ordinary room feel more expensive. Built-ins lend a sense of permanence to a space, especially in smaller homes, where storage that stays out of the floor plan is hard to come by.
