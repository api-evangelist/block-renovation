Your prewar condo has the ceiling height, plaster walls, and room proportions that newer buildings can't match. Bringing its kitchen, baths, and systems into this decade while keeping those bones is one of the better investments you can make in New York, and also one of the pricier ones. A modern renovation of a prewar NYC condo typically runs 20 to 30% more than the same scope in a postwar building.
