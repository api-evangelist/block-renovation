In Jackson Heights, the approvals set the schedule of a remodel more than the construction does, and they add real money to the budget. Most of the neighborhood is garden apartment complexes and pre-war co-ops, and a large part of it sits inside a landmarked historic district. Renovating here means working within small, fixed floor plans and getting two sets of approvals, the DOB's and usually a co-op board's.
