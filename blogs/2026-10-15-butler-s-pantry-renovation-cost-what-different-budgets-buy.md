Your kitchen cabinets are already full, and every party means clearing counter space for serving dishes, drinks, and the coffee maker. A butler's pantry gives all of that its own room, and a butler's pantry renovation costs anywhere from $8,000 for a cabinetry refresh to $40,000 or more for a full prep pantry with its own sink and appliances. That range comes down to how much you ask the room to do, so the first budget decision is which jobs you want it to take off the main kitchen.
