You saw selective demolition on a bid or a contractor's website, and you want to know whether it's a separate hire or something your general contractor already covers. For most home renovations it's the second one, and knowing the difference keeps you from paying two crews for one job. Selective demolition contractors remove one specific piece of your house, a bearing wall or a failed porch, while everything around it keeps standing.
