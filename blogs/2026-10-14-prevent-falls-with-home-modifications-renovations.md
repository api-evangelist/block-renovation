The best fall prevention starts with the home itself. A raised shower threshold, an awkward staircase, or an uneven floor can make ordinary routines more difficult than they need to be, especially as you get older. Addressing these hazards through thoughtful renovations allows your home to accommodate how you live, without compromising on the style that makes it yours.
