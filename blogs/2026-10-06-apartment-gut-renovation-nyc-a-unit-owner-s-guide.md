Own an apartment long enough and, sooner or later, you'll do a renovation that requires gutting. The catch in New York is that the walls, pipes, and hallways that gut has to pass through belong to the building. Roughly half the decisions in an apartment gut renovation in NYC get made by people who don't live in your unit.
